Baraka Social Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Toufik Ben handles information when you use Baraka Social Publisher to manage authorized Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok accounts.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, we may process account identifiers, usernames, display names, profile images, page identifiers, access tokens supplied by the connected platform, captions, images, videos, publication settings, scheduling information, platform responses, and technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the service. We do not request or store your social-media passwords or one-time verification codes.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate an authorized connection, prepare content, publish content at your direction, show publication status, prevent duplicate or abusive activity, respect platform limits, maintain security, and provide support. We do not sell personal information and do not use connected account data for unrelated advertising.

3. Third-party services

When you connect Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok, information is exchanged with that platform according to its own privacy policy and permissions. Media may be temporarily hosted or transferred through infrastructure required to publish it. We do not control the privacy practices, availability, or retention rules of third-party platforms.

4. Sharing

We share information only with the connected platform necessary to perform the action you request, with service providers that help operate the service under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law, security, or a valid legal process.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, to maintain records of requested publications, resolve disputes, protect the service, or comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of service-held information by contacting us. You may also revoke the service’s access from the settings of the relevant Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok account. Revoking access does not delete content already published on a platform; that content must be removed through the platform.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should protect your account credentials and never share passwords or verification codes with anyone.

7. Children

The service is not directed to children under the minimum age required by the applicable platform or local law. We do not knowingly collect information from children for independent use of the service.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy when the service, connected platforms, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new revision date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: dbdz200@gmail.com.
